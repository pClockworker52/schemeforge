## 1. Why a fourth round, and what it is for

Round 3 (docs/rounds/2026-10-23-euler1d-reconstruction_stateful.md) left Target A split across two families:

- **c-2ca8842812** (H-0013 proposer: THINC/BVD on all fields + a flip-count lock) passes A1, A2, A4, A6, A7, A8 and
  B1/B2/B5 on held-out data. It fails A3 (NF1 explicit 0.90 vs 0.95) and A5 (NF3 roughness 0.077 vs 0.002, 38 ×).
- **The engine's leaders** (THINC only on the entropy field, van Albada on the acoustic fields, smooth BVD weight)
  pass A5 at 1.7e-5 and halve A7a, but their RF1 shock median is 3.41 cells (A1 fails).

The working explanation (not yet tested): a THINC-sharpened *steady* shock locks to a few sub-cell positions, so the
wall force J(p_b) moves in steps as the back pressure moves the shock. Sharp steady shocks would then force a rough
objective. Round 4 tests that directly. Either outcome is a result:

- a candidate that passes all of Target A (promotion, and the hunch below refuted), or
- support for the trade-off across three scheme families (rounds 1–4), written up in the negative-result note.

This is the last Phase 3 round under the §11.4 kill criterion (4 rounds or ~2,000 candidates; ~1,550 used so far).

## 3. Pre-registration (fixed when this file is committed)

### 3.1 Hunch H-SR ("sharp steady shocks force a rough objective")

Registered with `sforge hunch add` before step 4 (id assigned then). Draft: ledger/drafts/r4_sharp_vs_smooth.yaml
(written together with this plan; content identical to the text here).

- **arena / slot:** euler1d / reconstruction_stateful (evidence from any Arena 1 slot counts, see the kill criterion).
- **lens:** contrarian. **author:** orchestrator/contrarian (a method hunch about the search space; it does not count
  for the swarm criterion S1).
- **statement:** No Arena 1 scheme reaches both a sharp steady-state-capable shock and a smooth steady objective: an
  RF1 shock-width median ≤ 2.6 cells (A1) and an NF3 roughness_J p90 ≤ 0.002 (A5) are not met together by any
  candidate whose NF1 Newton convergence is 1.00 (A4).
- **mechanism:** A reconstruction steep enough to put a shock into ≤ 2.6 cells resolves the shock position by
  snapping it to a small set of sub-cell positions; as p_b moves the exact shock continuously, the discrete shock
  jumps between those positions and J(p_b) acquires steps of the size of the sub-cell jump, which the second
  difference in roughness_J amplifies. Smooth objectives so far (van Albada, minmod, round-3 engine leaders) all come
  from schemes with RF1 shock ≥ 3.4 cells; sharp ones (superbee, MC, Koren, c-2ca8842812, round-2 near misses) all
  have NF3 roughness ≥ 0.077 or fail Newton.
- **predicted effect:**
  - every round-4 candidate with promo RF1 shock median ≤ 2.6 has NF3 roughness p90 > 0.002;
  - across all promo-evaluated Arena 1 candidates and zoo members with NF1 converged_impl = 1.00, the rank
    correlation between RF1 shock median and NF3 roughness p90 is negative (sharper → rougher), Spearman ρ ≤ −0.5.
- **kill criterion:** Refuted if any candidate, in any Arena 1 slot, has an ok promo run under judge v1.5 or later
  with RF1 shock_width median ≤ 2.6, NF3 roughness_J p90 ≤ 0.002 and NF1 converged_impl mean = 1.00. Otherwise
  supported "for the families searched" (it is never a proof for all schemes). The correlation clause is reported but
  does not decide the verdict.
- **known_relatives:** subcell resolution (Harten 1989); shock-capturing "staircase" effects on steady objectives in
  adjoint optimisation (to be cited from the literature note before the dossier, not claimed here); round 2 H-0011.

## 4. Steps

**Step 1 — Gate 3e records (WSL, Peter).** Close the round-3 hunches (D1), record the swarm outcome (D2), merge the
round-4 preparation branch. ~½ hour, no evaluations.

**Step 2 — Judge v1.5 and engine changes (cloud session prepares on the branch; WSL tags and rescored).**
1. Judge clean-up from the 2026-10-23 audit, first and alone: one slot table in the judge and one in the engine,
   stateless schemes run as stateful with the state passed through (removes the `.stateful` flag and
   `reconstruct_with_state`), one constraint checker per kind. **Acceptance: every zoo member's dev scores identical
   to v1.4** (checked in the cloud session on dev; on promo in WSL before the tag).
2. Then the content changes: accepted cases D3 in dev; zoo `thinc_bvd` EPS fix D4; the hard constraints gain a
   scaling check at ×1e-8 and ×1e8 on the probes (the round-3 EPS hole, B-0019, slipped through the ×10 metamorphic
   measurement); target `Ac` (§3.2) in promotion.
3. Engine selection for Target A work: parents and promo/breaker picks ranked by the number of failed Target A dev
   proxies (calibrated dev thresholds: R1 shock ≤ 2.7, B-0010 overshoot ≤ 1.1 × van Leer, N4 roughness ≤ 0.002,
   N1/N5/N6 explicit = 1, B-0002 order ≥ 1.8), then by the scalar score; duplicates (fingerprint distance < 1e-3 or
   identical dev aggregates to 3 digits) removed before top-k picks.
4. Engine budget: children whose request never reached the model do not count against `--budget` (gen 18 of round 3
   lost all 24 and still counted).
5. Proposer and hunch-generator prompts: every kill-criterion number must cite a run id; positivity fallback and
   relative-EPS traps added to docs/judge/euler1d_traps.md.
6. WSL: tag euler1d-v1.5, `sforge zoo eval` dev + promo, rescore reconstruction_stateful (and the other slots only
   if the step-2.1 acceptance check showed any difference).

**Step 3 — Register H-SR (WSL).** `sforge hunch add ledger/drafts/r4_sharp_vs_smooth.yaml`, before step 4.

**Step 4 — Round 4 (WSL, agents).** Slot reconstruction_stateful.
- Hunch-generator, 6 hunches, lenses as in round 3. Context adds: the two-family split of §1 and H-SR's mechanism
  *as the obstacle to beat*, without any suggested mechanism. At least two hunches must target the steady
  shock-position / roughness problem directly (the generator decides how).
- Proposers, 8 evaluations each; available parents for proposers: c-2ca8842812, c-a411e8accb, c-2637af56a6 (files
  given by id; proposers may start from scratch).
- Promo on the best 3 proposer candidates (deduplicated), then engine: 2 islands × 15 generations × 8 children
  (240) with the Target-A selection of step 2.3. Smaller than round 3 on purpose: round 3's best result came from a
  proposer, and the engine's island diversity collapsed under crossover.
- Promo + `promote --target A` and `--target Ac` on the deduplicated top 5; breakers on the deduplicated top 3.
- Round summary as in round 3, plus the H-SR evidence table (every promo-evaluated candidate with A4 = 1.00: RF1
  shock median, NF3 roughness p90) and the Spearman ρ.

**Step 5 — Gate (Peter).** Hunch verdicts including H-SR, A/A-c verdicts, quarantine, and the §3.3 next step.

## 6. Risks

- **The trade-off may be real** (H-SR supported). Then the round's value is the evidence, and §3.3 ends Target A. That
  is acceptable and pre-registered.
- **Positivity fixes may cost sharpness**; the breakers' nine crash cases make a fallback necessary for any
  promotable candidate (C9 / promo status).
- **The judge refactor (step 2.1) could change a score.** Its acceptance check is strict identity on the zoo; any
  difference stops step 2 until explained.
- **Usage limits** paused round 3 three times; the budget fix (2.4) prevents the loss, not the delay.
