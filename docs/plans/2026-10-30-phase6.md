# Phase 6 plan: Arena 2 judge and zoo, third-medium contact (written 2026-10-30)

Status: **approved by Peter 2026-10-30 with defaults D1–D5 and step 7 added.** Nothing below has been built yet. Based on PLAN §14, the tooling
spike (docs/spikes/2026-10-30-phase6-tooling.md) and what Arena 1 taught (docs/results/arena1-negative.md,
docs/judge/euler1d_traps.md). The decisions Peter needs to make are collected in §2.

## 1. Goal and gate

Build a judge for one component of third-medium contact (TMC), the strain energy of the fictitious medium that
fills the gap between bodies, plus a zoo of published media and a classical contact reference. No search in this
phase; that is Phase 7.

**Gate 6** (PLAN §14.8, sharpened by Arena 1):

1. Cases C0a, C0b, C1, C2 and C4 reproduced: TMC zoo against the exact solution and against a classical contact
   solution on a fine mesh. The judge's own error floor is measured and stated per metric, so that no Target
   threshold sits inside it.
2. The `judge-auditor` agent re-derives the references from the written spec, without seeing the code, and agrees.
3. Held-out families exist from the first version (Arena 1 lesson: dev results flatter).
4. The zoo front over the medium's stiffness scale is plotted (`docs/judge/contact_baseline.md`): softer media leak
   less force before contact but leave more residual gap and converge worse (PLAN §14.7). If the front does not show
   that trade-off, Phase 7 has no target and the phase stops there.
5. Target D thresholds pre-registered from the front, in their own commit, before any candidate exists.
6. Dev suite ≤ 60 s and ≤ 1 GB per candidate; judge tagged `contact-v1.0`.
7. Search readiness (step 7): the engine runs on the arena and rediscovers at least two zoo media from scratch;
   a first set of hunch drafts exists, with the closest published relative named for each.

Target E (friction, C3) and cases C5/C6 are not in this phase (§2, D4).

## 2. Decisions for Peter

| # | Decision | Default in this plan |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Arena name | `contact` (already reserved in `core/ledger.py`) |
| D2 | Hertz geometry | **Elastic cylinder (half model) on a rigid flat**, medium between them, its bottom fixed to the flat. The classical reference then needs only frictionless contact against a plane, which is simple and exact to implement. Two identical elastic bodies (PLAN §14.2) are needed only for Cattaneo–Mindlin (C3), which is deferred with Target E. Hertz holds for an elastic cylinder on a rigid flat with 1/E* = (1 − ν²)/E. |
| D3 | FElupe as a dependency | Pinned `felupe==11.3.0` in an optional dependency group `contact`, so Arena 1 users install nothing new. FElupe 12 removes the callback API the spike used. |
| D4 | Scope | Target D (frictionless) only. Friction (C3, C4 with μ > 0, Target E), ironing (C5) and self-contact (C6) follow in a later phase once D has a judge. C4 without friction (parasitic shear) stays in. |
| D5 | Candidate contract | `psi_medium(C, p)`: strain energy density of the medium as a `jax.numpy` function of C = FᵀF and judge parameters p. Frame-indifferent by construction; derivatives by JAX. Replaces PLAN §14.3's `psi_medium(F, p)` plus a frame check. The regularization slot `psi_reg` waits until the medium slot works (it needs a custom weak form). The contract sets the ceiling on what Phase 7 can invent: ψ(C) cannot see the contact direction, the medium's thickness or a history. Planned later slots, each its own judge version: a structural direction field (anisotropic media), a thickness measure, a state variable. |

## 3. Steps

Each step ends with tests green and a commit. Judge steps are judge commits (iron rule 2).

**Step 1. Arena skeleton and generic plumbing (1 session).**
- `src/schemeforge/arenas/contact/` with the same module roles as Arena 1: `cases.py`, `meshes.py`, `solve.py`
  (FElupe), `metrics.py`, `constraints.py`, `evaluate.py` with one `SLOT_TABLE` row (`medium`), `families.py`
  (held-out, seeded), `zoo/medium/`.
- The generic code still names `euler1d` in 25 places (`engine/objectives.py`, `probes.py`, `archive.py`,
  `core/dossier.py`, CLI and MCP defaults). Make them per-arena tables with no Arena 1 default. Arena 1 dev scores
  must stay identical (the v1.5 acceptance check: zoo dev before and after).
- Sandbox: candidates may still import only `jax.numpy`, `jax.lax` and `math`; FElupe stays inside the judge.
- Acceptance: `sforge eval zoo/medium/neo_hooke.py --arena contact --slot medium --suite smoke` runs one tiny case.

**Step 2. Exact small cases and the pressure measurement (1–2 sessions).**
- C0a gap closure (1D column through a medium-filled gap onto a rigid wall): ideal force is 0 until the gap closes,
  then the column's stiffness. Gives `leak_pre` and `gap_res` exactly.
- C0b simple shear of the medium alone: ideal shear stress 0. Gives the frictionless baseline of `shear_parasitic`.
- Contact pressure from the **nodal reactions on the rigid flat**, divided by each node's tributary length. This
  replaces the spike's rough thickness criterion: it is the quantity Hertz predicts, and summed it equals the total
  force by construction.
- Classical reference: frictionless penalty contact against the plane with a penalty sweep, extrapolated to
  infinite penalty, on the same meshes.
- Acceptance: C0a/C0b analytic values reproduced by the classical method to round-off, and the TMC zoo values
  reported against them.

**Step 3. Hertz and the patch test (1–2 sessions).**
- C2 Hertz (D2 geometry), a/R ≤ 0.1, flat at least 20 a wide: a, p0 and p(x) against the formula and against the fine
  classical solution, on three mesh densities and Q1/Q2 (`mesh_sens`).
- C1 contact patch test with non-matching meshes (Taylor–Papadopoulos): uniform pressure exactly.
- Measure the judge's error floor: classical method versus formula on each case, and its spread over meshes. This
  number goes into every metric's documentation.
- Acceptance: classical Hertz within 1 % of the formula on a and p0 at the finest mesh (the floor is reported,
  whatever it is); the dev Hertz case ≤ 15 s.

**Step 4. Constraints, metrics, held-out families (1 session).**
- Hard constraints on probe sets of C: finite energy, stress and tangent; ψ(I) = 0 and zero stress at C = I; positive
  energy for compressed states; unit scaling (ψ homogeneous of degree 1 in the judge's modulus parameter: the Arena 1
  v1.5 lesson); batch independence (the energy is evaluated one quadrature point at a time, as Arena 1 applies its
  candidates one item at a time); deterministic.
- Run-level constraints: min J of the medium > 0 (`min_J_medium`), Newton not diverged.
- Metrics of PLAN §14.4 except the friction ones; `max_step` by bisection only in promo (dev budget).
- Held-out families: Hertz with random R, E, ν, load and mesh offsets; patch tests with random non-matching meshes;
  gap closures with random gap and stiffness ratios. Seed outside the repo, as in Arena 1.

**Step 5. Zoo and the problem-existence check (1 session).**
- Zoo (PLAN §14.6, frictionless part): Wriggers–Schröder–Schwarz 2013 medium; Bluhm–Sigmund–Poulios 2021
  neo-Hookean with HuHu regularization; the FElupe example variants; the 2026 mixed formulation (arXiv 2606.28036) if
  it fits the contract; classical penalty/augmented-Lagrange contact as the non-TMC reference.
- Sweep each medium's stiffness scale γ0 over 4–5 decades; plot the front (`leak_pre` vs `gap_res`, coloured by
  `newton_iters`) in `docs/judge/contact_baseline.md`.
- Acceptance: the front shows the trade-off, or the phase stops with a short report (Gate 6 item 4).

**Step 6. Audit, Target D, tag (1 session).**
- `judge-auditor` blind re-implementation of C0a, C0b, C1 and C2 from `docs/judge/contact_spec.md`.
- Pre-register Target D (`docs/targets/D.md`) from the front: thresholds outside the measured error floor, held-out
  checks, bootstrap references, in its own commit.
- `sforge zoo eval --arena contact` on dev and promo; tag `contact-v1.0`; Gate 6 report for Peter.

**Step 7. Search readiness (1 session).** So that Gate 6 shows the search can work here, not only the judge.
- Engine tables for the arena: behaviour descriptors and grid (`engine/archive.py`), probes for fingerprints and
  duplicate detection (`engine/probes.py`), Target D dev screens (`engine/objectives.py`), objectives.
- Agent prompts: slot contract, traps file and zoo for the hunch-generator and proposer; the guard hook's workspace.
- Calibration run (as C1 in Arena 1): about 50 engine children seeded from the simplest zoo medium. Acceptance: at
  least two distinct zoo media rediscovered (fingerprint match), and the archive spreads over its grid.
- Hunch preview: the hunch-generator drafts six hunches for Target D (lenses as in Arena 1, including transfer from
  other fields). They are drafted, not registered or tested. For each, a literature check names the closest
  published relative and how the hunch differs from it, so the gate shows how new the ideas are before Phase 7 spends
  on them.

## 4. Carried over from Arena 1

- Held-out families and promotion by paired bootstraps from day one, not added later.
- Every judge version followed by a zoo check against the previous one (the v1.5/v1.6 acceptance records).
- Absolute tolerances are bugs: the unit-scaling constraint exists from the first version.
- Memory-aware parallelism: measure per-worker memory in step 3 and set the worker cap from it.
- One run id per claim; breakers on the zoo before the tag; the traps file starts now (`docs/judge/contact_traps.md`).

## 5. Effort and risks

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Steps 1–7 | 7–9 sessions (PLAN said 3–4; Arena 1 needed about twice its estimate) |
| LLM cost | none beyond the sessions (no search in this phase) |

- **The judge's error floor may be coarse.** Hertz is exact only for a/R → 0 on a half-space; the finite domain and
  mesh cost about 1e-3 to 1e-2. Targets have to sit above it, so differences between media smaller than that cannot
  be resolved. Step 3 measures it before anything depends on it.
- **The trade-off may not appear** (Gate 6 item 4). Then Phase 7 has nothing to search for, and the phase ends with
  that report; it is cheaper to learn this in step 5 than after a search.
- **Dev budget.** 60 s holds only with meshes of 3–6k DOF per case and `max_step` in promo (spike: 11.6 s for Hertz
  at 9.5k DOF).
- **FElupe API drift.** Pinned version; a later upgrade is its own judge commit with a zoo check.
- **Licence.** Settled: the repository is GPL-3.0-or-later (DECISIONS 2026-10-30), compatible with FElupe.
