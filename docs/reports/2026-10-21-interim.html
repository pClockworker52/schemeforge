4. Limiters we found, as functions φ(r)

Round-1 best (c-44f8015f87): φ(r) = smink(2r, 1 + ½ tanh(r − 1)), k = 10, with smink(a,b) = −ln(e−ka + e−kb)/k: compressive (2r) below 1, smooth cap rising to 1.5 above. Smooth ⇒ Newton converges; but only 60 % explicit convergence held-out.

5. Round 2: limiters with memory

New slot (judge v1.2): (φ, s′) = φs(r, s), one state value per cell and variable, fixed within a step, updated after it. Key lesson: freeze or filter the centred factor, not φ:

φ Δ₊ = ψ · (Δ₋ + Δ₊)/2 ,   ψ = 2φ(r)/(1+r)

Freezing φ fixes a multiple of the downwind difference → linearly unstable (blew up 19 steps after freezing). Freezing ψ fixes a multiple of the centred difference → stable. The best mechanism, a one-way ratchet after K₀ = 1000 steps, ψk+1 = min(ψ(r), ψk), converges on all 20 held-out nozzles; plain freezing fails on 2 of 20.
