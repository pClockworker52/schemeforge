## Candidate and attack

Characteristic amplitudes. Entropy field: ungated MUSCL/THINC BVD blend. Acoustic fields: BVD blend whose THINC part
is scaled by a quartic motion gate (D0 0.17, D1 2) from the previous-step state. Hard switches: primitive MUSCL in
strong expansions; fallback to the cell value when a face drops below 1 % of the cell's ρ or p.

Two fronts: overshoot (A7b), with RF2-type slowly moving Rankine–Hugoniot shocks over Mach, γ and shock-speed
fraction f (plus a warm two-shock collision); and Newton, in nozzle regimes the siblings did not cover (shock-free
supersonic nozzles, a shock pushed through the exit, a large inlet ratio). Overshoot depends strongly on γ and f: at
f = 0.2 (~0.1 cells per step, inside the gate's transition band) it is 6.0 × van Leer at γ 5/3, Mach 10; at
f = 0.1–0.15 it matches van Leer. Shock-free supersonic nozzles with a long low-Mach inlet are a new Newton failure.
